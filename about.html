About DOOMEY

Discover Skilled Hands. Build Great Things.

What we do

DOOMEY is an online marketplace that connects customers in Kenya with welders and metal fabricators. Customers can browse gates, window grills, staircases, carports, metal furniture and more, compare prices, see workshop locations on a map, and contact the fabricator directly.

For fabricators

Create a business profile, show your work with photos and videos, and receive orders and quotation requests from customers. Every advertisement is reviewed by our team before it goes public, and fabricators can apply for a VERIFIED badge after an identity check.

For customers

Find skilled people near you, view their previous work, and place an order or ask for a quotation. You pay the fabricator directly. DOOMEY does not collect or hold payments.
