Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider) and phone number.
  • Fabricator details: business name, skills, experience, workshop address and map location, photos and videos.
  • Verification documents: national ID or passport number, KRA PIN, business registration numbers and document photos, if you apply for verification.
  • Orders: products ordered, delivery location, notes and payment method chosen.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account, show listings and process orders.
  • To review advertisements and verification applications.
  • To send you order updates and notifications.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the marketplace safe.

3. Who can see it

  • Public: approved advertisements and fabricator business profiles, including workshop location.
  • Shared when you order: your name and phone number go to the fabricator you order from.
  • Private: verification documents are stored in a private area and are only visible to you and DOOMEY administrators.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and file storage provider, and the website is hosted on Vercel. These providers may process data on servers outside Kenya.

5. Keeping data safe

We use access rules so that users can only see data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it.

6. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Contact us using the details on our contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Location

The "Near me" feature uses your device location only when you tap the button and give permission. We do not store your location.

8. Visitor statistics

On our public pages we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular, so we can improve DOOMEY. Google may set cookies in your browser for this. We do not use it on your dashboard, orders, messages or payment pages. You can block it with your browser settings or by installing the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
